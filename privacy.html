Privacy policy

rclone backup · last updated 29 September 2026

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Google account that signed in to it. It is a private backup tool, not a service, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses the owner's own Google Drive. It lists, uploads and updates the backup copies of the owner's files there. It does not read or use any other Google data.

What it stores, and where

The sign-in token that Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer. The backed-up files go only to the owner's own Google Drive.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else. There are no analytics or adverts, and this website sets no cookies.

Google user data

rclone backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove its access to his Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email address shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
