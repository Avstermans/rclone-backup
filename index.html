rclone backup

A private backup tool. It is not a service, and it is not offered to anyone.

rclone backup is how one person backs up his own files. Every night, the free, open-source program rclone copies files from his own computer to his own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Copies new and changed files from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive.
  • Never deletes anything from Google Drive.
  • Signs in only to the owner's Google account, with the owner's permission.

Nobody else can sign in to it or use it.
